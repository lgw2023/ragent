Academic research figure, white background, flat 2D vector infographic, top-tier journal style, highly abstract, distilled, minimal text, elegant modular composition, thin outlines, balanced whitespace, clean arrows, no visual clutter.

Core goal:
Create a concise conceptual figure for the current Ragent algorithmic framework. The figure should communicate one main idea:

Professional documents are transformed into provenance-aware evidence units, materialized as an Entity-Relation-Chunk heterogeneous evidence graph, queried by cache-accelerated hybrid retrieval and evidence selection, and served through a decoupled read-only inference runtime that returns traceable answers.

This must look like a high-level research contribution figure, not a conventional system architecture diagram or deployment blueprint.

Current-version contributions that must be visually present:
- Entity, Relation, and original Chunk are unified into one provenance-aware heterogeneous evidence network.
- Query-time retrieval jointly uses chunk semantic retrieval, entity retrieval, relation retrieval, graph neighborhood expansion, query variant coverage, rerank, and final evidence selection.
- Engineering flow is decoupled into build-time construction, offline replay, evidence graph materialization, read-only inference, and cache acceleration.
- Cache acceleration must be one of the most visible compact motifs, showing repeated queries reusing keyword candidates / retrieval results without exposing cache internals.

Composition:
Use 5 large panels arranged left to right, plus one very thin bottom lifecycle ribbon. Each panel should be visually simple, with one bold title and at most 3 internal labels. Keep text sparse. Prefer symbols, shapes, colors, and spatial relationships over explanations.

Do not include a separate Flat Chunk RAG baseline panel.
